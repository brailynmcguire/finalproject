Welcome!

The purpose of this website is to compile recipes and my thoughts on them. I find that a lot of baking/cooking websites are slow and annyoing to use. And if I try to print out the recipe, it will often get lost or ruined. Therefore, I've created this website to organize my recipes and to simplify them to help me when I'm baking. I hope I will continue to use this website in the future and that it will improve my efficiency while baking and save paper. ♥
